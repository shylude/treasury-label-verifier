# Domain: Security Program Management & Oversight (20%)

## Exam objectives

- 5.1 Summarize elements of effective security governance
- 5.2 Explain elements of the risk management process
- 5.3 Explain the processes associated with third-party risk assessment and management
- 5.4 Summarize elements of effective security compliance
- 5.5 Explain types and purposes of audits and assessments
- 5.6 Given a scenario, implement security awareness practices

## Key concepts

- **Governance:** guidelines; policies (AUP, information security, business
  continuity, disaster recovery, incident response, SDLC, change management);
  standards (password, access control, physical, encryption); procedures
  (change management, onboarding/offboarding, playbooks). External
  considerations (regulatory, legal, industry, local/regional/national/global).
- **Governance structures:** boards, committees, government entities,
  centralized vs decentralized. Roles: owner, controller, processor,
  custodian/steward.

## Risk management

- Risk identification and assessment (ad hoc, recurring, one-time,
  continuous).
- Risk analysis: qualitative, quantitative, SLE, ALE, ARO, probability,
  likelihood, exposure factor, impact.
- Risk register: key risk indicators, risk owners, risk threshold.
- Risk tolerance and appetite (expansionary, conservative, neutral).
- Risk management strategies: transfer, accept (with/without exemption),
  avoid, mitigate.
- Risk reporting. Business impact analysis: RTO, RPO, MTTR, MTBF.

## Third-party risk

- Vendor assessment: penetration testing, right-to-audit clause, evidence
  of internal audits, independent assessments, supply chain analysis.
- Vendor selection: due diligence, conflict of interest.
- Agreements: SLA, MOA, MOU, MSA, WO/SOW, NDA, BPA.
- Vendor monitoring, questionnaires, rules of engagement.

## Compliance & audits

- Compliance reporting (internal, external); consequences of non-compliance
  (fines, sanctions, reputational damage, loss of license, contractual).
- Compliance monitoring: due diligence/care, attestation, internal/external,
  automation. Privacy: legal implications, data subject, controller vs
  processor, ownership, retention, right to be forgotten.
- Audits/assessments: internal (committee, self-assessment), external
  (regulatory, examinations, independent third-party). Penetration testing:
  physical, offensive, defensive, integrated; known/partially known/unknown
  environment; reconnaissance (passive/active).

## Security awareness

- Phishing campaigns (recognizing/reporting, anomalous behavior), security
  reminders, user guidance/training (policy handbooks, situational awareness,
  insider threat, password management, social engineering, operational
  security, hybrid/remote work). Development, execution, monitoring.
